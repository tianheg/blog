## `*` zero or more of the preceding match
## `+` one or more of the preceding match
## `[abc]` any one character of `a`, `b`, and `c`
## `(RX1|RX2)` either something that matches `RX1` or `RX2`
## `^` the start of the line
## `$` the end of the line
