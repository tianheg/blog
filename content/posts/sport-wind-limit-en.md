I worked overtime today, and went for a run in the evening. According to Zepp's plan, today's run was 11km. The last time I ran more than 10km was back in August.

Out the door, walk a stretch, stretch both arms out, twist the waist, loosen the ankles — getting ready for a long run. Music mixed with the voices of passersby around my ears; a lot of people come to the park for a walk, and I don't like crowds. I started running. While running, my head was very clear, and I was surprised at how clear-headed I was. I kept watching my body change: saw sweat run down my arms, felt the soreness in my knees shift, felt my breathing go from steady to hurried and from hurried back to steady. I didn't think much while running; I was just running. At 8km I thought that this was all I ran last time, and everything after this was a new record. As the end got closer I started checking my watch constantly, wanting to know how far the finish still was. I began to tire, I wanted to stop, but my body didn't stop. By the end there was only one goal left — finish the 11km.

No matter how tangled my goals were at the start, when I was worn out and still running, I thought: finish it soon, finish and rest soon, finish and rest and drink water soon. I'm going to die of thirst.

I thought: this time I pushed through a limit. Last time, running 8km, I didn't feel anything like this.

When I rested, I walked, until I reached a streetlamp — the light was very bright. Under the light lay a stretch of lawn; I sat down facing the lake. The rest after a run is always pleasant. Singing by my ears:

> In the vast sea of people
>
> too many brush past each other
>
> thankfully, your one line: I'll be sad with you
>
> I was never once moved
>
> I'm really trembling now
>
> I have never been freed
>
> I've tried to live anew
>
> I am never cold
>
> I try hard to love everything
>
> I've always stayed in the corner
>
> I long to see the lights
>
> the darker the sky
>
> the more the galaxy shows
>
> such a bright loneliness
>
> really breaks the heart

The lyrics suddenly touched me, and I understood: it's not without reason that I like this song.
